Material intelligence

Protection is built in layers.

Ripstop nylon, two thermal insulation layers and a fire-resistant layer combine into a lightweight, foldable system for outdoor and emergency use.

Four-layer protection system

01
Ripstop nylon
Lightweight, durable and water-resistant outer shell.
02
Thermal insulation
Reflects and helps retain body heat.
03
Thermal insulation
Additional insulation for improved warmth.
04
Fire-resistant fabric
Extra protection from heat and sparks.
